# Seven controlled signal extensions · November 2026

Freeze these historical comparisons before computing their outcomes. The earlier chapter 5 results and the sentiment follow-up were inspected. Keep one primary variant per question, report validation and replay separately and keep the references for deployment. A new endpoint, source or implementation changes the study version. No issuer, date, sign or candidate is selected by its later performance.

| Study | Candidate and comparison fixed for this release |
|---|---|
| H01 | Scale a trailing 756-session correlation matrix by 63-session asset standard deviations. Shrink each off-diagonal correlation halfway toward the mean pairwise correlation (LN3 constant-correlation target). Require 719/756 and 60/63 jointly complete CHF returns. Compare fixed equal-weight integrated variance with sample covariance, and separately replay long-only minimum variance at equal costs. |
| H02 | Fit a variance-targeted GARCH-type filter on the actual 756-session CHF grid, requiring 719 observed. Use a Gaussian quasi-loss on observed returns only. At a missing observation propagate expected squared innovation equal to its forecast variance. This is an approximate moment filter. Bound persistence to [0.50,0.995] and ARCH weight to [0,0.35]. Forecast and sum the variances of all sessions in the next month. Independently compare with rolling 63 and report EWMA on common dates. Reuse the capped 10% volatility policy. |
| H03 | Construct annual forward rates for maturities 1–5 from the Fed GSW continuously compounded zero-coupon yields. At month-end use the last complete curve strictly before the origin (at most seven calendar days old); it is a revised research snapshot. Predict the next month's IEF excess return using ridge (penalty 12) and five forward rates, standardized on expanding training rows with completed labels strictly before the decision and at least 36 rows. Compare against slope OLS. This monthly ETF adaptation differs from the annual bond study. |
| H04 | Use one predictor motivated by valuation: trailing 365-day SPY distributions divided by its contemporaneous USD chart close, observed strictly before the origin. Require a full year of price history and positive price. Estimate an expanding slope constrained nonnegative with a free intercept, shrink its prediction halfway to the training sample mean and floor the result at zero. Purge the latest contemporaneous label. Compare against the expanding mean excess return and replay the previous sign-based rule. |
| H05 | External portfolio diagnostic on four published US 2×3 sorts. For each stock size compare high book-to-market, high profitability, low investment and high 12–2 momentum, respectively, with their middle bucket. Primary metric is the monthly average of the four spreads across both sizes. Also report each component and size. Use 2014–18 and 2019–25. Gross USD series share stocks and CRSP ancestry. This different endpoint has no registered IC threshold. Only dated filings linked to historical securities could reopen the individual-stock strategy. |
| H06 | Transport the unchanged five-session continuous polarity rule to every classroom ticker except SPY and the already-inspected AAPL. Each comparison is a separate 50/50 instrument/SPY pair against its own anchor, with the original 2% TE ceiling and 0/10/25 bp costs. Start each phase at the anchor, restart after a missing outcome. Require 126 paired observations of 252 for covariance. Group paired utility gains by month before inference; count instruments and missing dates. Constituents come from the supplied classroom universe. This transfer check cannot establish message availability in real time. |
| H07 | Keep the registered 5 pp band. When outside it at decision close, target the first point on the straight line to equal weights at the 5 pp boundary. Use drifted positions, the next eligible opening proxy, the same reserve and costs as H07. Initial investment goes to equal weights. Compare to monthly rebalancing and report the existing full-reset 5 pp arm, turnover, terminal wealth, review breaches and withdrawals. |

For H01–H04 and H07 use 2016–19 validation and January 2020–October 2026 replay. H06 uses 2016–17 and January 2018–February 2020.

# Section 6.1 research design · 23 November 2026

Freeze the choices below before computing portfolio results. This is an illustrative CHF mandate for section 6.1. No participant supplied these limits.

- Decision at the final October venue session each year from 2019 through 2026. Report the latest frontier. Use the preceding seven one-year cohorts for a retrospective, independently funded comparison. Each begins with CHF 50,000 cash and a CHF 15,000 withdrawal in twelve months, no other flows. Use the intake cash ladder. Protect the reserve in CHF at par and apply risk budgets to the remaining CHF 35,000. No borrowing, derivatives or short sales. Permit unhedged USD exposure up to 90% of the initial sleeve, and each ETF up to 60%. No ethical exclusion in the base case. A named instrument exclusion binds, and an unresolved look-through requirement excludes the affected instrument. All-cash is permitted. A negative risk cap or an unfinanceable required risk exposure is infeasible.
- Use SPY (US equities), IEF (US Treasuries), GLD (gold). Require a positive last available adjusted price and observed USD/CHF at decision, and at least 60 jointly valid local-price and FX levels in the preceding 63 sessions per eligible ETF. This is a quote-availability proxy for liquidity. The assumed capacity is for the illustrative trade size. No measured bid/ask depth, broker fills or settlement guarantee. Lock the same eligible universe for every arm at a date.
- Estimate annual arithmetic CHF mean and covariance from 756 prior venue sessions ending one session before decision, at least 719 jointly valid daily CHF returns. Use complete joint observations for moments. For each of 199 training bootstrap paths sample joint blocks of 21 consecutive venue sessions with missing rows retained, then compute moments on observed rows. Use covariance of annualized bootstrap means for the uncertainty matrix. Fix radius 1, and 0 for the classical objective. CHF cash earns zero. Forecast horizon is one year.
- Charge 10 bp total on absolute ETF trades (illustrative 2 bp commission, 3 bp half-spread, 5 bp currency exchange). Sensitivities at zero and 25 bp. Weights and trades are fractions of pre-trade sleeve wealth. Sum of ETF positions, sleeve cash and fees equals one. Charge once on initial purchases. Cohorts hold to the end, report marked holdings plus cash without an assumed liquidation fee. No future trading or settlement proceeds fund the reserve. Model a rebalance from drifted holdings separately to verify accounting.
- Compare CHF cash, equal weights, 60/40 SPY/IEF, minimum variance within risky assets and equal risk contribution within risky assets. Build reference mixes on eligible instruments, renormalize surviving target weights and scale proportionally with CHF cash to satisfy risk, currency, holdings and funding limits. An empty reference holds CHF. Fully unconstrained minimum variance including zero-risk cash gives cash. Use the no-view Black–Litterman identity as a method check with a stated benchmark. No independently supported market-equilibrium portfolio or sentiment view on this universe.
- Sweep annual sleeve volatility limits of 0%, 4%, 8%, 12% and 16%. Maximize annual arithmetic net mean, or net mean minus the uncertainty penalty. Model costs on traded notional and keep cash as a decision outcome. Record objective, forecast risk, uncertainty, weights, fees, solver status, primal violations, volatility-limit dual and binding limits. Flag risk/return dominance across these computed candidates. Validate analytic two-asset cases and direct grid enumeration, the zero-radius identity, active and inactive duals and the zero-risk endpoint.
- At the latest date compare individual deterministic perturbations (SPY mean minus 2 percentage points, covariance shrunk halfway to diagonal, fee endpoints, USD cap 70%, instrument cap 45%). For 99 outer joint-block paths refit the moments, and use 99 inner joint-block draws for uncertainty on each path. Publish all attempted fits, weight and frontier quantiles, turnover, constraint violations, changes in fixed-mix/classical/robust ranking and denominators.
- For the historical comparison trade at the first complete adjusted CHF close in the first five sessions following the decision and mark at the last common close within five sessions of next October month-end. Use identical dates for all arms, no return before fill. Record unavailable observations. Each cohort resets to cash. Archive household annual net return before the external withdrawal, fees, funded payment, ending wealth and the paired differences. For a descriptive mean–variance score use mean minus twice sample variance of annual household returns (illustrative phi 4). Seven previously inspected cohorts cannot determine catalogue admission.

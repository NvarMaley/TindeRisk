# Section 6.2 · Dated client example, 23 November 2026

Use the October 2026 release of section 6.1. Present every feasible allocation at the 8% annual sleeve cap and repeat at 4% to illustrate lower risk capacity. CHF 50,000 initial household wealth, CHF 15,000 reserve, CHF 35,000 investable, CHF cash at zero, 10 bp modeled per ETF purchase or sale. Hard limits precede preferences. Keep all amounts and outputs specific to the example.

1. Use understood hypothetical choices C01 yes, C03 yes, C04 yes for a bounded one-year phi interval; also check comprehension missing, one-sided and contradictory states. Compare all option pairs on annual household and sleeve decimal net means and variances. Publish both endpoint differences, crossovers and exact numerical ties (tolerance 1e-9 in score). A switch, unsupported transfer from the CHF 1,000 interview stake, or sensitivity to inputs prevents a personal ranking. Compute Sharpe against CHF cash at par. Repeat the three common comparisons on the existing deterministic 6.1 sensitivities.
2. Show seven prior independently funded annual outcomes, starting fee, reserve and immediately liquid CHF, dated joint scenario loss from 4.2 and worst historical joint monthly shock. Conditional next-month range is the empirical 10th–90th percentiles of the selected holdings repriced on the preceding 36 complete calendar months in CHF, with entry fee and household base. Assess descriptive range coverage against the first realized month for the seven previous October decisions. Exclude the decision month from estimation.
3. For an illustrative goal, fix CHF 65,000 in October 2026 purchasing power in eight years, after the separate year-one CHF 15,000 bill. End-of-month savings CHF 100 nominal, held in CHF until each annual rebalance to the frozen feasible ETF weights. Rebalance on pre-trade investable wealth; fund purchases, sales and costs from the sleeve. Restrict weights at review dates; between reviews they drift. Use paired draws of 12 consecutive monthly USD ETF and USD/CHF gross returns (2,000 paths, seed 6202), drawn from the preceding 20 years ending September 2026. Use endpoints at a common recent CHF and ETF close, with at most four venue sessions of lag. Exclude blocks with incomplete months; never join across gaps. CHF reserve at par pays the bill separately at month 12. Report frequency of goal gaps, unconditional and conditional mean shortfall, wealth quantiles and dated funding trajectories. Report a zero prospective outcome count.
4. On the same draws compare 0%, 2% (base) and 4% annual inflation, CHF 250 monthly savings, a ten-year horizon and a CHF 55,000 real target separately. Reverse the order of the eight drawn years on the same path for sequence sensitivity. Also show break-even savings, time and target for deterministic zero market return with the same funded fees. No adjustment changes an allocation or its cap. Compare frequencies as scenario statistics conditional on the return generator. No extreme-value tail fit or probabilities for deterministic shocks.
5. Reverse-stress a parallel loss from 0 to 100% on all CHF ETF values at the bill date. Keep reserve and sleeve cash intact; securities count only if sellable and settled by the due time, net of 10 bp sale costs. Run CHF 15,000, CHF 22,000, and accelerated CHF 22,000 with no ETF sale proceeds. Use a disclosed flat-market path to month 12 including deposits; the accelerated payment is immediately after initial purchase and before deposits. Distinguish equality, unreachable failure, and already underfunded. Also report year-one resources across resampled paths.

# RiskMatch study register v1 · 22 November 2026

The seven rows in [studies.csv](studies.csv) freeze the first research comparisons. Each row gives the population, source, target, reference, candidates, primary score and smallest gain that would change the next decision. Every study adapts a course or published method to local data. The numerical course reproductions are recorded separately in notebook 1.2.3.

At each month end use only features released before the decision. Fit to outcomes whose availability is strictly earlier than the decision, including in validation folds. Hold all stocks in a month together. Use the first eligible later fill and evaluate the following monthly holding interval. A split is defined by the holding interval: omit boundary intervals crossing two blocks and purge any overlapping or unpublished training label. Refit transformations using training observations only. Compare candidates on common origins. An unavailable signal, failed fit or ineligible overlay takes the registered baseline on that date. Log each fallback, exposure, effective sample and direction of the paired difference. Data dated only to a day become usable the following session unless an earlier timestamp is verified.

For each row, the null is mean paired improvement at or below zero. Select the highest average validation gain among the listed challengers, resolving ties in listed order, then freeze that rule. In an independent sample, require a positive lower one-sided simultaneous confidence bound and a point estimate at least as large as the listed minimum. Use paired monthly blocks, initially three months with one- and six-month sensitivities, and control seven primary comparisons at 0.05 by Bonferroni. Insufficient independent blocks, unstable results or implementation failure retain the baseline. Record every additional variant under a dated exploratory amendment before accessing its outcomes. Gross forecast accuracy is a first gate. Adoption also requires a separately evaluated feasible portfolio, net of the same modeled 10 bp per traded notional in both arms, with 0 and 25 bp sensitivities and consistent FX charges. Keep the dated withdrawal funded throughout.

## Precision at the intended sample size

Run `python research/design_check.py` with the pinned NumPy version. The simulation uses 10,000 draws, seed 1201, 12 monthly evaluation observations, stationary Gaussian monthly paired gains with lag-one correlation 0.3, and pairwise correlation 0.5 across challengers. Each row declares an assumed monthly standard deviation in the units of its primary score. Select one candidate on validation and evaluate under the null and with the first candidate improved by the minimum gain. Output is the per-study false-positive rate, probability of detecting a positive gain and full model-based 95% interval width. The power calculation uses known variance and tests positivity only; additional adoption gates reduce power further. For H06 the 12-month calculation illustrates a hypothetical future feed. These are planning assumptions, not fitted estimates. All seven initial forward comparisons remain exploratory at twelve observations under this design.

## Implementation choices frozen before the replay

Use the [study register](../study_protocol.md), its dated calendars and the 3.2 arm-level eligibility manifest. The seven registered contrasts, validation tie order, minimum gains, costs and prospective gate take precedence over optional checklist extensions. Keep failed and unscored origins in the release. The historical panels have already been inspected. The earliest independent ETF outcome month is December 2026.

* H01 uses sample covariance and sklearn LedoitWolf's scaled-identity target on the same complete CHF rows. Freeze equal weights for the uncentered sum-of-squares target. Separately minimize variance long-only without expected-return estimates. H02 uses a 63-session rolling sample variance and normalized zero-mean EWMA with decay .94 on the same available grid, retaining the original session lags. The registered contiguous 756-session gate controls GARCH eligibility. The illustrative volatility policy caps exposure at 100% and targets 10% annual volatility, leaving unused capital in CHF cash at par. GJR and DCC are deferred by the register.
* H03 and H04 target the difference between compounded ETF and bill returns in USD. Fit expanding regressions with strictly earlier completed labels, including a one-month purge for the contemporaneous month-end label. H03 adds one standardized macro PC. H04 uses the registered fixed ridge penalty of one. Record chronological validation selection among OLS and ridge. Include equal-weight forecast combinations as exploratory controls, taking the reference if a component is unavailable. A central 80% range adds the historical 10th/90th percentiles of prior completed out-of-fold forecast errors after at least 24 errors. Uncalibrated ranges stay missing. Treat a positive excess forecast as a fully invested ETF sleeve and otherwise hold the USD bill proxy in an illustrative cost comparison.
* H05 preserves every excluded stock-model origin under the filing-publication gate. H06 uses the classroom AAPL/SPY returns and supplied event labels strictly as a replay. At each origin use a trailing 252-session covariance (minimum 126 valid rows), monthly covariance scaled by 21, risk aversion 4, equilibrium excess prior 4Σw, relative view P=(1,−1), signed Q=±1%, τ=.05 and Ω=PτΣP′. Apply the registered 2% annual tracking-error ceiling and long-only bounds. With an absent or neutral view use the anchor exactly. Both arms trade only at the first holding-session close, after receiving its return at the previous weights. Start each phase from the anchor. Missing outcomes interrupt drift and the next observed segment restarts from the anchor. Such resets limit economic interpretation.
* H07 uses a CHF 100,000 account, CHF 85,000 sleeve and CHF 15,000 segregated at inception for a single CHF 15,000 withdrawal at the end of month 12 of each phase. Reserve remains at par. Policies use the same target and start with the same funded cash. The dated first eligible opening proxy is the fill scenario, including delayed fills. Between reviews adjust units by total-return marks, price opening units via the chart open/close ratio and use the dated CHF fixing. The assumed all-in charge applies to actual purchases and sales, including the initial allocation, with no extra FX surcharge. Apply the quarterly rule in January, April, July and October. Evaluate the 5 percentage-point band on preceding-close weights and record post-trade drift at the review. Rerun 0, 10, 25, 50 and 100 bp for crossing sensitivity and 3/7 pp as exploratory band sensitivities. Economic replays are price proxies. No confirmed fills or client fee schedules exist.
* Controls use reference-minus-candidate losses, calendar moving blocks of 1/3/6 months with missing slots retained, fixed seed 501 and seven-way Bonferroni bounds. H03/H04 normalize by development reference MSE. Report raw squared error, QLIKE, pinball losses, coverage, clustered misses, volatility-state counts and a historical-start sensitivity. Use intercept-only ADF/KPSS plus trend sensitivity, HAC lag 3, and preserve negative or undefined scores. Joint OLS/ridge null simulations refit chronological forecasts after jointly resampling yield-spread and return innovations under zero predictability. Neither an independent decision nor a future execution is inferred from these retrospective comparisons.

## Forecast controls and decision

The return-forecast intervals target 80% coverage. Historical coverage was 65.9% for the H03 reference and 70.7% for the H04 reference; these bands are too narrow for client use. The release includes width, each tail's pinball loss, consecutive misses and prior-VIX/subperiod diagnostics. Small groups give imprecise coverage. The largest absolute reference errors occurred in September 2022 for bonds and March 2020 for equities. The equity reference ARCH-LM p-value was 0.015, consistent with changing error variance. Development ADF/KPSS tables include level/first difference, intercept/trend and lag choices. Inflation and industrial production enter as growth rates; a short sample and potentially persistent spread levels motivate the joint null simulation. There is no structural impulse response or cointegrating-system claim. [Calibration](results/interval_calibration.csv) · [Monthly errors](results/monthly_errors.png) · [Development diagnostics](results/development_diagnostics.csv)

Rolling and shrinkage covariance use their respective sample and maximum-likelihood normalizations. LedoitWolf shrinks toward a scaled identity; other published targets can give different answers. EWMA weights use their original session ages. HAC diagnostics use lag three, with symmetric DM for rolling versus EWMA, one-sided Clark–West for the eligible nested OLS contrasts, and moving-block comparisons throughout. The month blocks describe dispersion of the archived forecast losses conditional on their fitted histories. At the final H03 origin a separate training sensitivity masks its outcome and refits scaling, PCA and regression in 199 resamples for each block size. [Training refits](results/macro_refits.csv) An expanding-window joint null simulation reruns the H04 selection separately. Thin samples, many comparisons and dependence limit inference. ## Reproduce

With Python 3.12 and the project data available, run

```sh
python -m pip install -r requirements-experiments.txt
python research/experiments/run.py
python tests/test_experiments.py
```

The runner checks the input snapshots against their hashes, builds into a temporary directory and compares against the committed release. For a different numerical or graphics stack use `--output NEW_DIRECTORY` and compare the tables with suitable numerical tolerances. The split, execution proxy and readiness information originates in [the frozen 3.2 manifest](../../data/prepared_v1/samples.parquet). [Release hashes](results/manifest.json)

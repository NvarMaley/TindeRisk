How does a strategy become your card?

The USD portfolio universe includes eligible US stocks, equity ETFs and US Treasury ETFs. Each security needs 60 consecutive monthly total returns. USD cash is available when the mandate allows it.

  1. Filter

    Keep only the investments allowed by your mandate. A bond-only mandate excludes shares.

  2. Build

    Calculate how much to put into each holding. One approach seeks smaller fluctuations; another spreads risk across investments.

  3. Test

    Measure historical fluctuations and simulated shocks. An allocation that breaches a modeled hard limit cannot become a card.

  4. Present

    Rank compatible choices using your goals and preferences. Similar holdings are grouped. Some mandates can yield very few cards or none.

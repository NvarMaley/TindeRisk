## Four paths in the delivery audit

| Starting position | Expected behaviour | Verification and dependencies |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete questionnaire. The client prefers risk but ignores the amount of his next expense. | Maintain the response, seek clarification and leave the reserve and mandate to be confirmed. | Theoretical cases 2.2, 2.5, 2.7 and 3.3. Verify that no confirmed allowance is created. |
| Target out of range. The customer asks for a slice around 40% and the only authorized positions have a maximum risk verified below its low bound. | Show the application, the proven mark, the deviation and the feasible option subject to confirmation. If the calculation does not prove any bounds, display "undetermined". | Analytical case 5.2–5.4 and 5.7. Check the new consent before any change in 13.6. |
| Non-marketable assets. A title has sufficient history, but its deletion precedes the decision. | Keep its historical record, date the reason for exclusion and get a zero weight for the new proposal. | Before/after radiation 4.4–4.5 and 9.2 control of orders 5.8. |
| Review after selection. The customer already owns shares and requires less oil exposure. | Keep the old version, recalculate from positions held, display risk and charges before/after, then ask for confirmation before saving the revision. | Steps 8.4, 13.2–13.6 and 13.11. Verify that a refusal retains positions and that repeated confirmation does not duplicate any order. |

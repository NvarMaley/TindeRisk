# Guide to 41 cards · bank 2.0.0

Identifiers remain stable for this vintage. The tables follow the order of presentation. Right and right arrow select the first choice. Left and left arrow select the second. Each card accepts "I don't know / pass", recorded as unknown with no numerical contribution.

## Behavioural families

| Family | Common cards | Conditional cards | Authorized interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comprehension | C01 | C02 | Understand the bet before any inference. |
| Stated tolerance | T01 | T02 · T03 | Note the comfort and its possible offsets. |
| Certain outcome or gamble | G00 | G01 · G02 · G03 · G04 | Bound risk aversion (φ) and test its sensitivity. |
| Reference point | L00 | L01 · L02 · L03 · L04 · L05 | Identify an inversion according to the formulation. |
| Response to a loss | D01 | D02 · D03 · D04 · D05 | Distinguish liquidity, stability, anticipation and cover. |
| Flexibility | H01 | H02 | Document project flexibility. |
| Tracking | S01 · S03 | S02 | Document frequency, alerts and level of detail. |

G00 checks the comparability of choices and L00 collects the declared benchmark. Numerical follow-up questions require comprehension, and bets also require an accepted comparison. Only the gamble cards produce numerical bounds on φ. Amounts, percentages and priorities are self-reported.
